Safety posture Guardrail · 05
Useful when confident · conservative when not
Safety is not
a disclaimer.
It is a gate. Typed validation checks shape. Deterministic FastAPI policy checks eligibility. The model may increase caution; it cannot authorize its way around the product.

Visible uncertainty

Confidence, evidence, alternatives, and review state stay near the identity.

Safety before delight

Warnings appear before nutrition, facts, chat prompts, or recipes.

Fail closed

Unknown risk, review state, or expert verification removes recipes.

Chat inherits risk

A follow-up cannot negotiate a blocked result into permission.

The deliberate stop Specimen policy · 06
Mushrooms get the strictest path

The best recipe
is sometimes none.

A single photograph cannot establish edibility. EcoLens can support learning without turning resemblance into consumption advice.
